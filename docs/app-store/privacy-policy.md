# Privacy policy

_Effective October 17, 2026_

This policy explains what the Car Maintenance app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who
else handles it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what the app needs to
keep your cars, service history and documents in your account, we never sell your data or use it
for advertising, and there is no tracking.

Questions or requests: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers,
diagnostics, or usage analytics. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking
code.

Service reminders are worked out and scheduled on your phone. Whether reminders are on, whether
you have seen the introduction, and whether you allowed Ask to share data stay on your phone.

## Who else handles your data

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service
to us:

- **Supabase** stores your account, your cars, records and attached files, and sends sign-in
  emails.
- **PowerSync** keeps the copy of your data on your phone in sync with your account.
- **Anthropic** runs Claude, the AI model behind Ask, the quote checker and reading the odometer
  from a photo. It receives data only when you send a question, check a quote or send an odometer
  photo, and only after you allow it in the app (see below).
- **NHTSA** (the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) decodes a VIN you enter into
  the car's year, make and model. Only the VIN is sent. Recalls and owner complaints are fetched
  from NHTSA by model and year, not by anything about you.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or
let anyone use it to track you across apps and websites.

## Ask and the quote checker

Ask answers questions about your car, the quote checker reads photos of shop quotes, and the car
page can read the miles from a photo of your odometer, using Claude, an AI model made by
Anthropic. Before any of them sends anything, the app tells you what will
be sent and asks for your permission. You can turn this off at any time in **Account → Privacy**,
and the rest of the app keeps working.

When you ask a question, Anthropic receives the question, your car's details (including its VIN
and nickname), its maintenance schedule, your odometer readings and service records (including
costs, shops and your notes), and the recalls and owner complaints for its model. When you check a
quote or read your odometer from a photo, it receives that photo. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to
train its models and generally deletes it within 30 days, though it may keep data flagged for
breaking its usage policies longer. We keep your Ask
conversations in your account until you delete your account.

Ask explains your car's records. It is not a mechanic and cannot inspect your car. For anything
about safety, have the car checked by a professional.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app
(**Account → Delete account**), we delete your account and everything stored for it from our
database straight away: cars, odometer readings, service records, fill-ups, warranties, renewal dates, your schedule items, reminders
and Ask conversations. Attached files are deleted from storage within a few hours. Copies in our
providers' routine backups are overwritten on their normal schedule, within **[BACKUP PERIOD]**.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and take your data.** Account → Export or import data gives you a zip with everything, as
  open CSV and JSON files and the original photos and documents.
- **Correct or delete it.** Edit or delete warranties, renewal dates, your schedule items and documents in the app, or delete your
  whole account in Account. To correct or remove anything else, email us.
- **Stop sharing with Anthropic.** Turn it off in Account → Privacy.
- **Notifications, camera and photos** are only used if you allow them, and can be turned off in
  your phone's Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia or Colorado), you may have further
rights to know, correct or delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against
for using them. Email us to use any of them; we will respond within the time the law requires.

## Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can read and change only its own data, enforced by
the database itself. Your phone keeps a copy so the app works offline. It is deleted when you
delete your account, and when you sign out, unless changes are still waiting to upload; those stay
until you next sign in.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal
information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data
